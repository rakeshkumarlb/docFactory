# AI-Driven Job Matching Platform Overview

## Application name

AI-Driven Job Matching Platform for Palestine

## Purpose

To enhance labor market efficiency by providing real-time data integration, standardizing job postings, and facilitating employment for job seekers.

## Business overview

Part of the 'Youth Economic Empowerment in Palestine (YEP)' program, aiming to enhance Palestinian young people's employability and economic empowerment by integrating multiple job sources and government databases to optimize job matching and provide analytics.

## Business owner

Ministry of Labor (MoL) and Palestinian Employment Fund (PEF)

## Target users

- Job Seekers (Youth 15-29)
- Employers (Private sector enterprises)
- External Job Sites (Job portals)
- System Administrators (MoL and PEF staff)
- Government Officials (Decision-makers, Policy makers)

## Key capabilities

- User Management (Job Seekers, Employers, External Job Sites, Admins)
- Job Posting and Management
- AI-Driven Matching Engine (Skill-based, NLP, Recommender systems)
- Integration Framework (APIs for external job sites and government databases)
- Reporting and Analytics (Dashboards for labor market trends)
- Multilingual Support (Arabic and English)

## Out of scope

- Implementation of the full Labor Market Information System (LMIS)
- Hardware procurement for hosting the solution
- Integration with systems beyond those specified in the document
- Training of end-users (addressed in Phase Two)
- Long-term support and maintenance
- Mobile application development

## Business criticality

Core component of the future Labor Market Information System (LMIS) to enhance labor market efficiency.

## Lifecycle status

Under development (Draft SRS v1.0)

## Go live date

TBD

## Technology summary

Web-based application with responsive design, utilizing AI/ML frameworks for matching algorithms and RESTful APIs for integration.

## Related systems

- MoL databases
- PEF databases
- External job sites (e.g., jobs.ps)
- Government databases
- Educational institutions databases
