1. Support Model

JobMatch uses a three-level support model. Level 1 is the service desk (business days 08:00 to 17:00 local time) which logs and resolves simple issues. Level 2 is Platform Operations which diagnoses technical faults and is on call around the clock for critical incidents. Level 3 is the engineering team that owns the faulty component.

3. Escalation Path

Service Desk Agent, then Platform Operations Engineer, then the owning engineering team lead, then the Platform Team Lead, then the Operations Manager, and finally the Director of the Employment Services Directorate for incidents that affect the public.
